Elevation in the terrain pilot

The close-up view covers Oslo–Lillestrøm, the mountains around Aurland and the low-relief landscape around Herning. The rest of the map uses the existing hex elevations. All municipal figures and boundaries keep their existing sources.

Heights come from Copernicus GLO-30 Public, the 2021 distribution of cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs. This is a surface model: Vegetation and buildings can affect the heights. It is not a bare-earth lidar survey or a map of individual buildings. Heights refer to EGM2008.

The source grid is about 30 m. In these areas, latitude spacing is one arc second, about 31 m; longitude spacing is 1.5 arc seconds south of 60°N and two arc seconds north of it. Reprojection and interpolation put these measurements into NordicCiv’s existing map projection. The output does not add detail below the source resolution.

The vertical scale is 1×. Shading follows slope and a fixed light from the northwest. Landscape colours follow elevation and slope; they are not a new land-cover survey. Existing roads, rivers, coasts and municipal boundaries retain their original resolution. Close-up terrain does not add surveyed buildings, trees or infrastructure.
